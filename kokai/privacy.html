Ko Kai privacy policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Ko Kai is an app for learning the Thai alphabet, made by Thomas Avé.

Data collection

Ko Kai does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app has no access to the internet, no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.

Your settings and your progress in unfinished practice rounds are stored only on your device, so the app can remember them between sessions. They never leave your device, and they are deleted when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
